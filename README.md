## How I approach architecture

- **Start with the problem:** understand users, requirements and system constraints before choosing tools.
- **Make decisions explainable:** clarify component responsibilities, interfaces and important trade-offs.
- **Test uncertainty early:** use focused proofs of concept and evaluation to inform implementation.
- **Build for the surrounding system:** connect models, services and developer workflows with maintainability in mind.
- **Help teams continue the work:** share technical context, mentor contributors and make handoffs understandable.
